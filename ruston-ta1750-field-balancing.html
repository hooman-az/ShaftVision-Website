Ruston TA-1750 Gas Turbine — Field Balancing with the Rotor in Place

A field-balancing intervention carried out on an installed Ruston TA-1750 gas turbine. The rotor remained in the machine; access was achieved by removing the upper half of the air-compressor casing.

13 mm/sOverall vibration before balancing · RMS
3 mm/sOverall vibration after balancing · RMS
≈77%Recorded vibration reduction

Engineering challenge

The work involved balancing the gas-turbine rotor without extracting it from the installed machine. Rather than removing the rotor for workshop balancing, the intervention was planned around the machine's actual installed configuration and the dynamic behaviour observed at its bearing system.

Field approach

  • The upper half of the air-compressor casing was removed to provide access for the intervention.
  • The rotor remained in its installed position; it was not removed from the machine for workshop balancing.
  • The balancing assessment and correction approach were based on the measured dynamic behaviour of the installed bearing system.
  • Vibration was checked after the intervention to document the resulting machine response.
Recorded result

Overall vibration reduced from 13 mm/s RMS before the intervention to 3 mm/s RMS afterward. This is an approximately 77% reduction for this specific machine and operating condition.

This is a machine-specific case result, not a guaranteed outcome or a universal performance benchmark. Detailed balancing weights, correction planes and plant operating records are not published on this page.
