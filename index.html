Field engineering case study

Ruston TA-1750 gas turbine — field balancing.

A practical field intervention carried out on the installed turbine, without removing the rotor from the machine.

The balancing work was performed with the turbine remaining in its installed configuration. The shaft/rotor was not removed for workshop balancing; the correction approach was developed around the actual dynamic behaviour of the machine’s installed bearing system.

The work documented a reduction in overall vibration from 13 mm/s RMS before balancing to 3 mm/s RMS after the intervention.

The upper half of the air-compressor casing was removed during the work. This case demonstrates a field-based engineering approach to a specific Ruston TA-1750 machine and operating condition.
