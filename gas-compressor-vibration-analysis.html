Gas Compressor Vibration Analysis & Diagnostics

Gas compressors are critical assets in oil and gas facilities. Reliable vibration assessment requires more than a single overall value: engineers consider machine design, bearing type, operating condition, measurement location, sensor technology and changes over time.

Independent engineering support — scope agreed per case

ShaftVision offers specialist rotating-machinery diagnostic consulting based on the information and measurements supplied for each case. This service page describes engineering support; it does not mean the ShaftVision software analyzes AC vibration, orbit, waveform or full-spectrum data.

Why gas compressor vibration needs context

Compressor trains may include high-speed rotors, fluid-film bearings, seals, couplings, gear units and complex process conditions. A useful diagnostic review considers the machine configuration and measurement system before interpreting vibration levels or trends. Operating changes, transient events and maintenance history can be important to the assessment.

Information useful for an engineering review

  • Compressor type, train arrangement, driver and driven equipment details.
  • Bearing and sensor arrangement, measurement locations and channel definitions.
  • Operating speed, load, process condition and event timeline.
  • Available vibration measurements, trends and supporting maintenance history.
  • The specific question the site team needs answered.
