Industrial Vibration Analysis & Rotating Machinery Diagnostics

Vibration data is valuable only when it is interpreted in the context of the machine, its operating condition, measurement locations and history. ShaftVision provides independent, case-based engineering support for vibration analysis and rotating-machinery diagnostics, with a focus on industrial and oil and gas equipment.

Engineering consulting — separate from the ShaftVision software

This page describes specialist engineering review and consulting. It does not imply that ShaftVision – Core Fault Diagnostics processes AC vibration waveforms, orbit plots or full-spectrum files. The software is specifically designed for Average Shaft Centerline (DC Position) assessment from proximity-probe DC readings.

What a useful vibration assessment considers

A sound diagnostic review starts by confirming the machine configuration and the measurement system. Depending on the case, the review may consider vibration trends and spectra supplied by the site, shaft-relative measurements, operating speed and load, process conditions, bearing and support arrangement, recent maintenance and the timing of observed changes. A single overall vibration value is rarely enough to establish a root cause on its own.

How to discuss a case

Start with the machine type, the symptom or engineering question, operating speed and load, measurement locations, and a short description of the available vibration data. You can then agree what information is useful to share for a focused review. Do not send confidential plant information unless you are authorized to disclose it.
