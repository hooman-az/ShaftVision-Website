ShaftVision – Core Fault Diagnostics

ShaftVision is a focused engineering application for Average Shaft Centerline (DC Position) analysis. It helps rotating-equipment professionals convert X-Y eddy-current proximity-probe DC readings into a reference-based view of average shaft position within fluid-film bearing clearance.

Designed for one clearly defined measurement domain

The software focuses on average shaft centerline / DC position. It is not an AC vibration analyzer, orbit plot package, waveform viewer or full-spectrum diagnostic system. Keeping this boundary clear helps engineers select the correct data and interpret the result in its proper measurement context.

What the software is designed to do

Reference-based position

Record the mechanical rest condition and compare it with operating DC readings using a consistent reference.

X-Y probe geometry

Enter the installed probe orientation and sensitivity so the two channel readings can be interpreted in the agreed coordinate system.

Bearing-clearance context

Review calculated radial shaft position in relation to the entered bearing clearance geometry.

Engineering reporting

Present the measurement inputs and analysis outcome in a structured report for technical review and maintenance communication.

What information is required?

  • X and Y proximity-probe DC readings at the confirmed rest/reference condition.
  • X and Y DC readings at the operating condition or selected measurement points.
  • Installed probe angles and applicable probe sensitivity.
  • Bearing-clearance dimensions and the mechanical reference geometry.

A practical analysis workflow

  1. Establish the reference. Confirm the rest readings and the coordinate convention before calculating movement.
  2. Enter operating measurements. Record the running DC readings for the required measurement points.
  3. Review the position. Assess the calculated average shaft centerline in relation to the bearing clearance.
  4. Document the result. Generate an engineering report for review and communication.

Who is it for?

ShaftVision is intended for rotating-equipment engineers, machinery diagnosticians, reliability and maintenance professionals who work with fluid-film journal bearings and proximity-probe DC position measurements.

Frequently asked questions

Does ShaftVision analyze AC vibration or orbit data?

No. ShaftVision is focused on Average Shaft Centerline (DC Position) analysis. It is not an AC vibration analyzer, orbit plot package, waveform viewer or full-spectrum analyzer.

What measurements are needed for an analysis?

The workflow uses X and Y proximity-probe DC readings at the confirmed rest/reference condition and at the operating condition, together with probe angles, applicable sensitivity and bearing-clearance geometry.

Why is the rest condition important?

The confirmed mechanical rest readings establish the reference for comparing operating DC position. The reference must be defined from the actual measurement setup; it should not be replaced by an assumed universal shaft position.

Can DC shaft position replace vibration measurements?

No. Average shaft centerline describes the shaft's average position relative to a reference. It does not provide vibration amplitude, orbit shape or dynamic vibration severity. Those require appropriate dynamic measurements and analysis.

Who can use ShaftVision?

The software is intended for rotating-equipment engineers, machinery diagnosticians, reliability specialists and maintenance professionals who work with fluid-film journal bearings and proximity-probe DC position measurements.
